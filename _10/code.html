Philately

فيلاتيلية

FR: Philatélie EN: Philately

The study and collection of postage stamps and postal history. It involves the careful examination of paper, watermarks, perforations, and printing methods.

border_horizontal

Perforation

التسنين

FR: Perforation EN: Perforation

Rows of small holes punched between stamps to make them easy to separate. Measured by the number of holes in a 2 centimeter span using a perforation gauge.

water_drop

Watermark

العلامة المائية

FR: Filigrane EN: Watermark

A faint design pressed into the paper during manufacture, visible when held to light or placed in watermark fluid. Often used to prevent counterfeiting.

layers

Gum

الصمغ

FR: Gomme EN: Gum

The adhesive substance applied to the back of a postage stamp. Its condition (mint never hinged, hinged, no gum) significantly impacts a stamp's value.

local_post_office

Cancellation

الختم

FR: Oblitérations EN: Cancellation

A postal marking applied over a stamp to deface it and prevent its reuse. Cancellations can provide valuable historical and geographic context.

mail

First Day Cover

مظروف اليوم الأول

FR: Premier Jour EN: FDC

An envelope or postcard bearing a new postage stamp that is postmarked on its first day of official issue, often featuring decorative cachets.

print

Overprint

توشيح

FR: Surcharge EN: Overprint

Additional text or graphics printed over a completed postage stamp. Commonly used to alter the face value, indicate a change in currency, or for special events.
